About

Hi, I’m Usha.

Product designer for enterprise, AI and design systems.

How I got here

I studied architecture for five years. Drawing buildings taught me to start with how people move through a space, then give it structure, then the detail.

In 2022 I moved into product design. Since then I’ve designed telecom marketing platforms, an ESG reporting copilot, AI agents for customer success and the design systems behind them.

How I work

Decision first

Lead with the answer people need. Put the data one level down.

People stay in charge

AI drafts and suggests. People approve, with the reason in view.

Research before screens

Interviews and field visits until the real problem is clear.
