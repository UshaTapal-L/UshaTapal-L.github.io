How I work

From problem to shipped

  1. Frame the problem. Stakeholder interviews and field visits until the real job is clear.
  2. Let data argue. Usage data and heuristic reviews over opinions.
  3. Map every journey. Happy paths, edge cases and hand-offs.
  4. Decide, and write down why. One clear direction, with the trade-off named.
  5. Ship and measure. Usability tests, then the metric that matters.

How I lead

Ownership, not hand-offs

  • I own the problem, not just the screens. I sit with product managers, engineers and data scientists from framing to launch, and review built screens until they match.
  • I set the bar for quality. I created usability-testing routines at zeeproc and run the design system at 6D.
  • I grow designers. I mentor juniors on layout, components and visual consistency.
  • I make the call. When data and opinions disagree, I decide, write down why, and measure it.
