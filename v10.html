Enterprise · ESG reporting · 2024 Client work

RIU

A 0-to-1 redesign of an ESG and regulatory reporting platform for compliance teams.

Role
Lead product designer. Research, strategy, interaction and visual design, and the field app.
Problem
Dense compliance data across spreadsheets; teams couldn’t see what to do next.
Decision
Progressive disclosure, and navigation rebuilt from usage data rather than the feature list.
Outcome
+40% satisfaction · 35% fewer steps for field inspections.

Approach

How I make decisions.

01

Frame the real problem

Interviews and field visits until the job to be done is clear, before any screen exists.

02

Let evidence decide

Usage data and usability findings settle debates. Opinions start them.

03

Name the trade-off

Every direction gives something up. I write down what, and why it’s worth it.

04

Own the outcome

I stay through build and launch, review what ships, and measure what changed.

Key decisions

The calls, and what they cost.

    Made the recommendation the main screen, with charts as supporting detail.

  2. Magik agents · 6D
