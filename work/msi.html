My Site Inspection: designing with field teams, on site

I redesigned the My Site Inspection (MSI) mobile app for field teams, using on-site usability testing to cut the effort of an inspection.

Role
Product Designer, research owner
Timeline
2024
Company
RIU (Reporting Intelligence Unit)
Platform
Mobile app
35%fewer task steps and less physical effort

01Problem

Field teams use MSI to record site inspections while on their feet and on site. The existing app asked for too many steps, which is costly when someone is working in the field.

Who the inspectors are, and what made the old app hard to use on site.

02Research

I owned the research end to end: stakeholder workshops, user interviews and on-site field usability testing with the people doing the inspections.

How many sessions, where, and the most surprising thing you saw in the field.

03Define

Your design question, for example: "How might we let inspectors complete an inspection with fewer steps and less effort?"

04Design

Key design decisions, such as merged steps, larger touch targets, or one-handed use, and why.

05Test and iterate

What you tested with field teams after the redesign and what changed.

06Build and handoff

How the design was handed off and shipped.

07Outcome

  • 35% reduction in task steps and physical effort for field teams.

08Reflection

2 to 3 sentences: what you would do differently, and which question from this project you want to study in your Master's.
