Magik: from dense dashboards to an AI agent workspace

I led UX strategy and product design to turn Magik, a legacy Customer Value Management platform for telecom operators, into an agentic AI marketing automation product.

Role
Product Designer, UX lead
Timeline
Mar 2025 to present
Clients
Etisalat, MTN Ghana, BICS, Smart Communications
Team
Product managers, data scientists, engineering leads
30%reduction in campaign setup and launch time

01Problem

Magik helped telecom marketing teams plan and run customer campaigns. It was built around dense dashboards, and it competed with enterprise tools such as Adobe Experience Manager. Marketers had plenty of data, but turning it into a campaign decision was slow.

A concrete example of the old workflow: how many screens or steps it took to launch one campaign.

02Research

I combined user research, product analytics and a competitive analysis of enterprise marketing platforms.

Number and type of users you spoke to, and your top 3 findings.

Marketers needed clear decisions, not more data.

03Define

The insight reframed the product. The question was no longer "how do we show more data?" but "how might we hand marketers a clear, explainable decision they can act on, while keeping them in control?"

04Design

I designed a Decision-First UX framework that replaced dense dashboards with natural-language AI recommendations. Around it, I designed an AI agent workspace where marketers review, edit and launch multi-channel campaigns.

  • Recommendations first: the AI's suggestion and its reasoning lead, and supporting data sits one level down.
  • Human in the loop: every agent action can be reviewed and edited before launch.
  • Low-code visual guardrails: marketers set limits visually, so the agent cannot act outside them.

05Test and iterate

Because these workflows are high-stakes, I validated them with product analytics, user feedback loops and usability testing before rollout.

One change you made because of testing (before and after).

06Build and handoff

I built and scaled a centralised design system in Figma, with design tokens and reusable component libraries. It improved UI consistency and reduced friction in the design-to-development handoff. I worked closely with product managers, data scientists and engineering leads on roadmap prioritisation.

07Outcome

  • 30% reduction in campaign setup and launch time.
  • Moved Magik from a legacy CVM tool to an agentic AI product competing with enterprise marketing suites.
