Magik: from dense dashboards to an AI agent workspace

I led UX strategy and product design to turn Magik, a legacy Customer Value Management platform used by telecom operators, into an agentic AI marketing automation product.

Role
Product Designer, UX lead
Timeline
Mar 2025 to present
Clients
Etisalat, MTN Ghana, BICS, Smart
Team
PMs, data scientists, engineering leads
Result
30%faster campaign setup and launch

01Context

Magik helped telecom marketing teams plan and run customer campaigns. It was built around dense dashboards and competed with enterprise tools such as Adobe Experience Manager. Marketers had plenty of data, but turning it into a campaign decision was slow.

A concrete example of the old workflow: how many screens or steps it took to launch one campaign.

02Research

I combined user research, product analytics and a competitive analysis of enterprise marketing platforms.

Number and type of users you spoke to, and your top 3 findings.

03Insight

Marketers needed clear decisions, not more data.

The question became: how might we hand marketers a clear, explainable decision they can act on, while keeping them in control?

04Design

I designed a Decision-First UX framework that replaced dense dashboards with natural-language AI recommendations, and an AI agent workspace where marketers review, edit and launch multi-channel campaigns.

  • Recommendation first. The AI's suggestion and its reasoning lead; supporting data sits one level down.
  • Human in the loop. Every agent action can be reviewed and edited before launch.
  • Visual guardrails. Marketers set limits in a low-code editor, so the agent cannot act outside them.

To keep this consistent across modules, I built and scaled a design system in Figma with design tokens and reusable component libraries, which reduced friction in design-to-development handoff.

05Testing

These workflows are high-stakes, so I validated them with product analytics, user feedback loops and usability testing before rollout.

One change you made because of testing, with before and after.

06Outcome

  • 30% reduction in campaign setup and launch time.
  • Moved Magik from a legacy CVM tool to an agentic AI product competing with enterprise marketing suites.
